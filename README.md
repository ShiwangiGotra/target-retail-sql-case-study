# 🛍️ Target Retail Business Case Study – SQL Analytics

This project presents a comprehensive business case study on Target’s e-commerce operations, using SQL to uncover insights into customer behavior, sales performance, logistics, and payment trends.

## 📌 Objective
To analyze customer orders and transactions to identify business patterns, seasonal demand, logistics performance, and payment preferences.

## 📁 Dataset Tables
- `customers`
- `orders`
- `order_items`
- `payments`
- `sellers`

## 🔍 Key Analyses
- 🗓️ Time range and growth trends in orders  
- 🌍 Customer distribution by city and state  
- 🛒 Monthly seasonality and peak ordering hours  
- 🚚 Delivery delays vs. estimates  
- 💸 Payment methods & installment behavior  
- 📦 Sales and freight performance by region

## 🛠️ Skills & Tools
- SQL (BigQuery syntax)
- Joins, CTEs, Window Functions
- Aggregations & Date-Time Functions
- Business Interpretation & Recommendations

## 📊 Sample Insights
- Afternoon was the most active time for placing orders  
- Certain states showed consistently faster-than-estimated deliveries  
- Freight and delivery times varied widely by region  
- Credit cards were the dominant payment method

## ✨ Outcome
This project enabled a deep understanding of Target’s operational and customer behavior patterns, which can inform marketing, logistics, and CX strategies.
